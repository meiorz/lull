Things to know

  • Hold Alt and click a paused GIF to play it. A GIF that Lull is not allowed to read is hidden behind a dashed outline instead; the same click shows it.
  • A video you start yourself is never paused again.
  • Printing always uses the page's original colours.
  • If one site looks wrong, open Lull on it, choose More, then Simple invert.
  • To use Lull on files on this computer, open the browser's extension page for Lull and switch on "Allow access to file URLs".

What Lull cannot change

  • The browser's own pages (new tab, settings, extension store) and its PDF viewer. Browsers do not let extensions edit them.
  • Pages that draw everything onto a canvas, such as some document editors. Simple invert works on these.
  • Background pictures set by a site's style sheet. They are shown as they are.
  • Animations that a page runs from script. Lull stops style-sheet animations, autoplay and GIFs.

Privacy

Lull has no account, no analytics and no remote server. Your settings stay in this browser. The only requests it makes are for style sheets and pictures that the page you are on already uses, so that it can read their colours. Requests to other sites carry no cookies.
